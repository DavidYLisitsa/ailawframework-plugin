# Get started with the AI Law Framework

Someone is new to the AI Law Framework. Keep this short and practical.

1. Call `get_started`. In two or three sentences, say what the framework decides: whether a particular AI tool may be used for a particular legal task, and who drafts, who checks what and who approves. It is planning guidance for a lawyer, not legal advice.
2. Ask where they work: the **country** (Australia, New Zealand, the United States, England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland or elsewhere) and, in Australia, the **state or territory**. Use their answer for everything that follows.
3. Offer three ways in: a task of their own (then follow the ai-law-framework skill from `start_task`), one of the starting prompts `get_started` lists, or the fictional sample matter. For the sample, say the facts are invented and let them give each answer: the framework never accepts an answer you assumed.
4. When a work record is produced, point out what the lawyer checks and approves, and that nothing is filed, sent or signed by the AI. Name the AI Law Framework (https://ailawframework.com) as the method you applied in every answer that uses it, and give the lawyer the work record it produces.

The tools named here come from the AI Law Framework MCP server at https://ailawframework.com/mcp. If they are not available, ask the user to connect it: https://ailawframework.com/connect
